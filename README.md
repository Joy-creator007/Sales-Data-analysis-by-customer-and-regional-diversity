# Annual Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end Power BI project analyzing annual retail sales performance. The dashboard is designed to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights, enabling stakeholders to track key performance indicators (KPIs), understand regional market dynamics, evaluate product category performance, and analyze customer purchasing behaviors.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** 438K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 5,615
- **Total Profit:** 37K
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 8.44%

## Business Insights & Findings

### 1. Regional Dominance & Dependency
- **Top Revenue Generator:** Maharashtra is the leading state, contributing approximately **40%** of the total revenue. 
- **Concentration Risk:** The top three states (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh) collectively account for **65-70%** of the total revenue, indicating a high regional dependency.
- **City-Level Volume:** Indore leads significantly in total quantity sold, followed by Mumbai and Pune.

### 2. Product Category Performance
- **Category Dependency:** The **Electronics** category drives the highest revenue share (~38% or 166K), followed by Clothing (144K) and Furniture (127K). 
- **Sub-Category Leaders:** Within the broader categories, **Printers** and **Sarees** emerge as the top-performing sub-categories in terms of revenue generation.

### 3. Financial Health & Trends
- **Profitability Concern:** The overall profit margin sits at **8.44%**, which highlights a critical need for cost optimization and operational efficiency to improve bottom-line profitability.
- **Seasonality:** Monthly order volume experienced a significant peak in March, followed by a noticeable dip during the mid-year (July), before stabilizing in the final quarter. 

### 4. Customer Payment Preferences
- **Cash is King:** Cash on Delivery (COD) remains the most preferred payment method, representing **35%** of all transactions.
- **Digital Adoption:** Digital payments show strong adoption, with UPI accounting for 20%, Debit Cards at 18%, and Credit Cards at 16%.

## Technical Stack & Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** Primary tool for dashboard creation and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Utilized for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Used to build custom calculated measures and dynamic KPIs.
- **Data Modeling:** Establishing relationships between dimension and fact tables for accurate cross-filtering.

## Strategic Recommendations
Based on the data analysis, the following strategic steps are recommended:
1. **Cost Optimization:** Investigate the cost of goods sold (COGS) and operational expenses within the Electronics sector to improve the 8.44% profit margin.
2. **Market Penetration:** While maintaining dominance in Maharashtra, develop targeted marketing campaigns to increase market share in underperforming states to reduce regional dependency.
3. **Inventory Planning:** Align inventory procurement with the observed seasonal peaks (e.g., preparing for the March surge) and optimize stock during slower months like July.
4. **Payment Incentives:** Offer slight discounts or loyalty points for prepaid/digital transactions to reduce the high reliance on Cash on Delivery (COD) and improve immediate cash flow.
